    Mechanic booking questions

    How do I book a mechanic online in Prayagraj?

    Choose bike or car, select the required service and enter your name, mobile number and service location. You can request immediate assistance or a scheduled visit through the MechWay booking page.

    Do I need to log in or create an account to book?

    No. Customer mechanic bookings and restoration enquiries do not require a login or signup.

    Where is doorstep mechanic service available?

    MechWay provides bike and car mechanic assistance in Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh. Share your exact location when booking so the team can confirm availability for your area.

    Does the visiting charge include every repair?

    The visiting charge is shown before confirmation. Additional repair labour, replacement parts and consumables may cost extra. The team confirms additional work and its cost with you before proceeding.

    Can I request restoration for an old bike or car?

    Yes. Submit a restoration request with your vehicle details, photos and preferred budget. The team reviews the condition, documentation and parts availability before providing an estimate.
