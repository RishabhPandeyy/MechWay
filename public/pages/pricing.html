The MechWay Promise

How your warranty works

Clear terms for your service, from payment to follow-up.

What's covered
If the same issue returns because of the service work, contact us within 7 days of completion. We will arrange a free revisit to check and correct that work.
What's not covered
Spare parts, replacement parts and their costs are excluded. Unrelated faults and new service work are also not covered.
Payment eligibility
Pay the mechanic's service bill through the MechWay payment link issued for your booking. Keep the booking reference and payment receipt.
How to claim
Contact MechWay within 7 days of service completion. Share your booking reference, payment receipt and details of the recurring issue.

Need to know

Warranty questions

When does the 7-day warranty period start?

The 7-day service warranty starts when the covered service work is completed.

How do I receive the 7-day service warranty?

Pay your mechanic's service bill through the MechWay payment link issued for your booking. The service warranty lasts 7 days from service completion.

Are spare parts covered by this warranty?

No. This is a service-only warranty. Spare parts, replacement parts and their cost are not included.

What happens if the same service issue returns?

Contact MechWay within 7 days of service completion. Share your booking reference and payment receipt so we can arrange a free revisit to check and correct the covered service work.
