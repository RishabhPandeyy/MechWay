Our Process

How We Restore
Your Bike

A proven step-by-step system built over hundreds of restorations — so nothing is missed and everything is perfect.

Phase 01

Free Inspection & Quote

We pick up or inspect your bike, photograph every detail, assess all components and give you a complete written quote with zero obligations.

Phase 02

Full Strip-Down

Every component is removed, labelled and photographed. Bolts, nuts and small parts go into labelled bags. Nothing gets lost, nothing is assumed.

Phase 03

Assessment & Parts Order

Every part is measured, graded and a decision made — restore, replace or custom fabricate. Parts ordered from verified suppliers only.

Phase 04

Engine & Mechanical Work

Engine rebuilt, frame repaired, suspension refreshed, brakes renewed. All mechanical work completed and signed off before paint begins.

Phase 05

Paint, Chrome & Finishing

Bodywork painted, chrome polished, seat upholstered. Electrical system wired fresh. Every detail fitted with care.

Phase 06

Test Ride & Handover

Road tested, tuned, photographed and delivered. You get a full restoration report, all old parts returned, and a 90-day warranty.

Bike restoration and customization: frequently asked questions

Can I request bike restoration from anywhere in India?

MechWay accepts bike restoration and customization enquiries from across India.

You can submit an enquiry from any Indian state. Include your city and PIN code so the team can review project feasibility and transport options. MechWay is based in Prayagraj; a nationwide enquiry service does not mean a workshop in every city. Confirm pickup, delivery costs and scheduling before sending your vehicle.

What is the difference between bike restoration and customization?

Restoration brings a motorcycle back towards its original condition; customization changes its appearance or comfort to suit your preferences. A project can combine both: repair the engine and wiring first, then agree on paint, chrome and seat details. MechWay reviews your bike, reference photos and budget before recommending a scope of work.

How much does bike restoration cost in India?

The indicative MechWay budgets on this page are ₹15,000–₹35,000 for a selected bike refresh, ₹40,000–₹1.2 lakh for a substantial rebuild and ₹1.2 lakh or more for a collector project. These are planning ranges, not fixed offers. Condition, parts, paint and agreed work determine the final inspection-based estimate; transport costs are confirmed separately.

Rust, engine wear, missing original components, paint preparation and parts sourcing all affect cost. Our indicative restoration budgets distinguish a selected refresh from a substantial rebuild or collector project. They are planning ranges; the final estimate follows inspection and an agreed parts and labour scope.

Can I enquire about Royal Enfield Bullet or Yamaha RX100 restoration?

For a Royal Enfield Bullet, Yamaha RX100 or another older motorcycle, start with its condition, missing parts and the finish you want to preserve. Engine, fuel system, rust, brakes and wiring need assessment before deciding between selected repairs and a complete rebuild. Model acceptance depends on parts availability and inspection.

Can I choose custom bike paint and seat upholstery?

Share references for tank colours, graphics, seat stitching and trim. Agree on materials, finish and fit before work starts. If you have a cafe racer or other custom build in mind, send the concept for a feasibility review; the final scope depends on the motorcycle and intended use.

How long does a motorcycle restoration take?

The timeline depends on the agreed work, vehicle condition and parts sourcing. A substantial rebuild involves assessment, dismantling, mechanical work, finishing and testing. Ask for a project-specific schedule after inspection; rare parts or additional approved work can extend it.

Is pickup and delivery available outside Prayagraj?

Outstation transport is reviewed for your location and project. Confirm collection and delivery availability, charges, arrangements and timing with MechWay before dispatch. Free nationwide pickup is not included by default.

What should I share to get a bike restoration or customization quote?

Include the make, model, approximate year, whether the bike starts, known faults and photos of both sides, the engine, tank and rusted areas. Add a short starting or running video if available, your location, target budget and the original or custom finish you want. This helps the team discuss suitable work before a detailed inspection.
