Engine Rebuild

Rust removal, de-pitting and mirror-finish chrome polishing that makes your exhaust, rims and fittings gleam.

  • Exhaust pipe restoration

Old looms cause fires and mystery faults. We replace every wire with fresh loom, connectors and fuses.

Our Process

How We Restore
Your Bike

A proven step-by-step system built over hundreds of restorations — so nothing is missed and everything is perfect.

Phase 01

Free Inspection & Quote

We pick up or inspect your bike, photograph every detail, assess all components and give you a complete written quote with zero obligations.

Phase 02

Full Strip-Down

Every component is removed, labelled and photographed. Bolts, nuts and small parts go into labelled bags. Nothing gets lost, nothing is assumed.

Phase 03

Assessment & Parts Order

Every part is measured, graded and a decision made — restore, replace or custom fabricate. Parts ordered from verified suppliers only.

Phase 04

Engine & Mechanical Work

Engine rebuilt, frame repaired, suspension refreshed, brakes renewed. All mechanical work completed and signed off before paint begins.

Phase 05

Paint, Chrome & Finishing

Bodywork painted, chrome polished, seat upholstered. Electrical system wired fresh. Every detail fitted with care.

Phase 06

Test Ride & Handover

Road tested, tuned, photographed and delivered. You get a full restoration report, all old parts returned, and a 90-day warranty.

FAQ

Questions We
Hear Every Day

How long does a full restoration take?

A full restoration typically takes 30–60 days depending on the bike's condition, parts availability and the scope of work. We give you a timeline estimate upfront and update you every week with photos.

Do you restore all bike brands?

Yes — Royal Enfield, Hero, Honda, Bajaj, TVS, Yamaha, Kawasaki, Suzuki, KTM, Jawa and more. We have experience with both vintage and modern motorcycles across all Indian and imported brands.

Can I see progress during restoration?

Absolutely. We send you photo and video updates every 5–7 days via WhatsApp. You can visit the workshop at any time by appointment. Nothing is done without your knowledge.

Do I need to bring my bike to you?

No. We offer free pickup and delivery within Prayagraj. Just call or WhatsApp us and we'll arrange collection at a time that suits you.

What warranty do you offer?

All Full Restore and Custom Build packages carry a 90-day warranty on workmanship. If anything we repaired fails within 90 days, we fix it at no charge. Custom Builds come with a 1-year warranty.

Can I choose a custom paint scheme?

Yes — we do everything from exact factory colour matching to fully custom designs. Bring us reference images, colours you like, or just describe your vision. Our painter will create a design mockup for approval before a drop of paint is sprayed.
