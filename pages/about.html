Our Story

Redefining Roadside Repair

Founded by Rishabh Pandey

Connect on LinkedIn

MechWay was founded with a simple yet powerful idea: auto repair should be convenient, transparent, and customer-focused. Our founder grew tired of seeing people waste their entire day at traditional garages.

Stranded on the roadside or busy at work – car trouble always strikes at the worst times. By bringing certified mechanics directly to you, we eliminate towing hassles and long waits.

Our focus is simple: diagnose clearly, quote honestly, repair carefully, and let customers pay only after the job is done.
