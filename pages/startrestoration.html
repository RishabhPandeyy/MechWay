      Understanding Restoration

      What Is Motorcycle Restoration?

      Motorcycle restoration is the process of bringing an old, classic or vintage motorcycle back to a mechanically reliable, visually exceptional and historically appropriate condition.

      • Engine & Gearbox
      • Frame & Chassis
      • Suspension System
      • Braking System
      • Wheels & Tyres
      • Electrical System
      • Fuel System
      • Paint & Finishing
      • Chrome & Plating
      • Seat & Trim

      "Restoration is not simply about making an old motorcycle look new. Our objective is to restore mechanical reliability, visual quality and authenticity according to the customer's selected restoration specification."

      Comprehensive Scope

      Restoration Services

      Specialized mechanical, structural and cosmetic craftsmanship across every system.

      Engine & Gearbox

      Complete teardown, measurement, machining and reassembly with precision tools.

      • Cylinder, piston & crankshaft work
      • Valves, bearings, seals & gaskets
      • Clutch, gearbox & carburettor rebuild

      Frame & Chassis

      Structural integrity restoration, rust removal, alignment and protective finishing.

      • Frame inspection & alignment
      • Rust treatment & structural repair
      • Swingarm & frame coat finishing

      Suspension

      Front fork rebuilding, rear shock overhaul and bushing renewal for factory ride.

      • Fork seals, oil & bushings
      • Rear shock rebuild/replacement
      • Damper & spring calibration

      Brakes

      Complete braking system renewal for modern safety standards on classic builds.

      • Brake shoe / pad replacement
      • Drum & disc resurfacing
      • Master cylinder & line renewal

      Wheels & Tyres

      Rim polishing, spoke replacement, wheel truing, alignment and tyre fitment.

      • Rim restoration & spoke truing
      • Hub restoration & new bearings
      • Period-correct tyre replacement

      Electrical

      Full wiring harness replacement, charging systems, lighting and instrument restore.

      • Complete new wiring loom
      • Ignition, battery & charging
      • Switchgear & instrument cluster

      Metal prep, dent removal, epoxy priming and factory period-correct paint finishing.

      • Tank, fender & panel restore
      • Paint stripping & rust priming
      • Factory or custom clear coat

      Chrome & Metal

      • Exhaust & rim chrome plating
      • Zinc & nickel component plating
      • Mirror-finish metal polishing

      New high-density foam, custom stitching and weatherproof period-correct upholstery.

      • Base pan repair or fabrication
      • New foam shaping
      • Leather or vinyl stitching

      Vehicle restoration questions

      Can MechWay restore both bikes and cars?

      Yes. The restoration request form accepts motorcycles and cars. The team reviews vehicle condition, structural integrity, documents and parts availability before confirming the work it can undertake.

      What restoration work can I request?

      You can request a complete restoration, engine rebuild, paint and body work, chrome plating or other work. Describe your priorities so the team can prepare a vehicle-specific estimate.

      How much does bike or car restoration cost?

      The restoration pricing guide shows indicative budgets for different levels of work. Your written estimate depends on inspection, required parts, labour and finishing. Pickup, transport and applicable taxes are confirmed before work begins.

      Which photos and documents should I upload?

      Upload five images: the vehicle RC, front view, rear view, left side and right side. Include your registration number, vehicle condition and requested work so the team can review your enquiry.

      Can I arrange pickup from outside Prayagraj?

      Share your pickup location in the request form. The team confirms whether transport is available from your location and quotes its cost separately before arranging pickup.

      What happens after I submit a restoration request?

      The MechWay team reviews your vehicle details and contacts you as soon as possible about inspection and next steps. For routine doorstep repairs instead of restoration, book a bike or car mechanic.
